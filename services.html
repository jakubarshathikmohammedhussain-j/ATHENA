Athena services

Three tools that save a team hours every week, with sources you can check.

Competitor monitor

A weekly briefing on your company and up to two rivals: what changed, new risks and moves worth a response. Delivered by email, with a web copy you can download as PDF.

Supply chain risk radar

List your suppliers and their locations. Athena checks weather and news for each one, scores the risk, shows how much spend is exposed and suggests what to do. Can also arrive weekly by email.

Open the radar

Document intelligence

Upload a contract, policy or annual report. Get a summary, key dates, obligations and risks, then ask questions. Every answer cites the page, and each quote is checked against the page text.
